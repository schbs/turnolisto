## Post de LinkedIn en inglés (≤ 1.300 caracteres)

How many appointments does a clinic lose every week because someone didn't show up?

I built TurnoListo, a WhatsApp assistant for a dental clinic (demo project with fictional data):

✅ Books, reschedules and cancels against the real schedule, even from a voice note.
✅ Sends reminders 48 h, 24 h and 3 h before, with a one-tap Confirm button.
✅ When someone cancels, offers the slot to the first person on the waitlist.
✅ Emergencies or "I want to talk to a person" go straight to the front desk, without relying on the AI.
✅ The front desk sees everything in one panel: confirmations, handoffs and the weekly schedule.

What I cared about most was testing it properly: 16 business conversations with real Gemini answers, tests for the
scheduling rules and accessibility checks. One of those conversations caught a real bug: the model booked a patient
twice at the same time. Now a system rule prevents it, whatever the AI does.

Measured: mobile Lighthouse 98/100/100/100, 2.2 s median reply and about USD 0.003 per conversation.

Stack: WhatsApp Cloud API, Gemini, Next.js, Supabase and Google Calendar.
